02

Beliefs.

I like thinking deeply about things, questioning what I'm told, and forming my own opinions.

I'm interested in how people think, why things work the way they do, and ideas that actually make me stop and think.

I don't believe being smart means knowing everything.

For me, it's being curious enough to keep asking better questions.

03

The Things I Do.

I’ve tried a lot of things over the years. Some stayed with me, some disappeared after a while, and some taught me something even though I never got good at them.

I like that about myself.

I don't think everything I try has to become a talent, a hobby, or something I can show people. Sometimes I just want to know what something feels like, what I can learn from it, or whether it belongs in my life at all.

There were things I started because I was curious. Things I kept doing because they made me happy. And a few things I left behind because I realized they weren't really for me.

I’m still trying things. Still learning. Still changing my mind sometimes.

I think that's probably going to be a part of me for a long time.

04

A Favourites.

BLUE

Probably my favourite colour. I’ve liked it for as long as I can remember.

I’ve grown, changed, and gone through different phases of life, but somehow, I still come back to blue.

MILK & WATER

My usual drinks. Nothing fancy. I just like them.

There’s something nice about having a few simple things you never really get tired of.

MUSIC

Kendrick is one of my favourites, but that's definitely not the whole picture.

My taste changes a lot. Different genres, different artists, different sounds, depending on what feels right.

Some songs are just songs.

Others become attached to people, places, and moments you didn't know you'd remember.

GAMES

Free Fire, Roblox, Chess.com.

I've played a lot of games over the years. Some stayed for a long time, some didn't.

But when I look back, I don't remember every match or every win.

I remember the people, the laughs, and the nights that somehow became memories.

ANIMALS

I like pretty much every animal. I’ve got dogs and bunnies, so those are probably the ones I'm around the most.

They become part of your everyday life so naturally that you don't really notice it happening.

Until you realize they've become part of home.

05

The People I Got Lucky With.

I’ve met some genuinely great people, both in-game and in real life.

Different places, different stories, different kinds of friendships, but somehow they all became part of my life.

I don’t take that for granted.

I feel pretty blessed to have the people I have.

06

Little Things.

Not everything about me needs a whole explanation.

I like small things that make everyday life better, good conversations, random laughs, late-night gaming, discovering something new, listening to music, spending time with people I care about, and having things that feel like mine.

I also appreciate simple things more than complicated ones.

Sometimes the little things are enough.

07

Things I’m Still Figuring Out.

I don't have everything figured out, and honestly, I don't think I'm supposed to.

There are things I still don't understand about myself, things I change my mind about, and things I'm still trying to become better at.

Some days I feel like I know where I'm going. Other days, I really don't.

I'm still growing into the person I'm going to be.

And I think it's okay not to have the whole story figured out yet.

08

Things I Like.

I like things that genuinely interest me, not just things that look impressive.

Games, music, animals, technology, interesting conversations, discovering random things online, and anything that gives me something new to explore.

My interests change over time, and that's part of it.

I like having more than one side to myself.

09

Memories.

Some moments don't feel important while you're living them.

They're just normal days, random conversations, late nights, stupid jokes, games with people you happened to meet, and little things you never thought you'd miss.

Then time passes.

Some people stayed. Some left. Some I had to leave behind. Some came into my life for a while, taught me something I needed to learn, and eventually became a memory.

Not every memory is a happy one either. There were difficult days, unfortunate moments, mistakes, and things I wish had gone differently.

But even those days became part of who I am.

I think that's what memories really are. Pieces of people, places, and moments that once meant something to us.

Some are still close. Some feel far away now.

But I wouldn't want to erase any of them.

A page kept for someone kind

The kindest girl I’ve ever met through a game, and genuinely one of the purest souls I’ve come across.

What makes her different is the way her kindness feels natural. She doesn't need a reason to be good to someone, and she doesn't seem to do it for attention either. That kind of person is rare.

I admire the softness in her character, the way she can make someone feel comfortable just by being herself. There’s something really genuine about that.

I don't have one perfect moment that explains why she matters. Sometimes it's not one big memory. It’s the collection of small things that slowly make someone important.

Meeting her through a game was random.
Keeping the memory of her isn't.

If I had to describe her in three words:

Kind. Genuine. Pure.

And if she ever reads this, I’d want her to know one simple thing:

I'm genuinely glad I met you.

I hope you never lose the part of yourself that makes you this kind. The world has enough people trying to be impressive. Staying genuinely good is something else.
